PROGRESSION: PLANET COLLECTION SHEET. A neat collectible lineup like a cosy game album: eight tiny planetoids floating in two tidy rows on a plain soft cream background, each isolated with a gentle shadow and a faint glow, all in the same soft pastel vinyl-toy style, each a different single idea: a lantern planet, a library planet made of stacked books, a tea-house planet with steam, a cocoa-volcano planet with marshmallow clouds, a wind-chime planet, a glass greenhouse planet, a bubble planet with floating soap bubbles, a sleepy moon planet with a night-cap. The mascot sits on the second planet, waving its whole body happily. Two of the planets in the bottom row are shown as soft grey "not yet discovered" silhouettes. No text, no labels.
