PROGRESSION: THE STAR MAP. A dreamy top-down-ish view of a small pastel galaxy drawn like a cosy illustrated map, but rendered in the same soft 3D vinyl style: the mascot's home planet in the lower left, and a winding path of small glowing stepping-stars leading across the sky to about eight tiny themed planets (each a different single idea: a lantern planet, a library planet made of stacked books, a tea-house planet, a planet with a little cocoa volcano, a wind-chime planet, a greenhouse planet, a bubble planet, a sleepy moon). The first three planets along the path are lit, colourful and alive; the rest are softly dimmed silhouettes waiting to be discovered. The mascot rides a tiny paper boat on the starry path near the newest lit planet. No text, no labels.
