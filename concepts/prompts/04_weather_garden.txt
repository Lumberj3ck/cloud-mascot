Idea: THE WEATHER GARDEN. A dreamy little walled garden at soft morning light where weather grows like plants: tiny rainbows arching out of flower pots, sun-flowers whose heads are glowing little suns, raindrop-shaped glass buds, puffy baby-cloud shrubs. The mascot hovers just above a sprouting seedling and gently rains a few tiny sparkling droplets onto it (each completed habit waters the garden). Stepping stones, a small watering can, a white picket fence, soft greens with lavender and peach. Hopeful, nurturing.
