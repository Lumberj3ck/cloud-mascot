Idea: THE BALLOON JOURNEY. The mascot rides in the woven basket of a small pastel hot-air balloon (balloon striped lavender and cream) drifting over a soft patchwork landscape of rolling pastel hills, tiny round trees, a winding river and little villages far below, at golden morning light. A dotted path of tiny glowing markers trails behind the balloon across the land (the streak as distance travelled), with a little flag at the next milestone ahead. A few birds and soft clouds. Adventurous yet calm.
