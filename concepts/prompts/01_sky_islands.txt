Idea: SKY ISLANDS AT GOLDEN HOUR. The mascot's home is a small floating island made of soft puffy clouds high above a sea of clouds at sunset (peach, lavender and soft gold). On the island: a tiny round cushion bed, a little glowing lantern, a few glowing star-shaped flowers. Other small floating islands drift in the distance, linked by faint rainbow bridges; a couple of tiny hot-air balloons. Airy, dreamy, cosy.
