Idea: A TINY PLANET IN A PASTEL COSMOS. The mascot sits on top of its own small round planetoid covered in soft velvety lavender-and-mint moss, with a tiny cushion, a little brass telescope and a few glowing mushroom lamps; the planet's curvature is clearly visible. Around it: a soft lavender-peach nebula, a small ringed planet, a crescent moon, scattered twinkling stars, and a faint constellation made of glowing dots connected by thin light lines (the habit-streak constellation). Gentle, wondrous, cosy.
