PROGRESSION: HABIT PLANETS. The mascot sits on its central home planetoid; around it, on gentle glowing orbit lines, four small companion planets, each representing one of the user's habits and each grown to a different stage: a little ocean planet with a fountain and droplets (drinking water, well grown), a library planet of stacked books with a reading lamp (reading, medium), a meadow planet with stretching flowers and a tiny swing (movement, small and young), and a sleepy moon planet with a cushion and nightcap stars (sleep, fully grown and glowing). Each companion planet's size and glow shows how well that habit is kept. Soft lavender-indigo space, warm highlights. No text.
