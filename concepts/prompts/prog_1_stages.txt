PROGRESSION: HOME PLANET GROWTH STAGES. One wide image split into four equal vertical panels left to right (thin cream gutters, no labels, no numbers), same camera angle, same deep-lavender starry background, showing the mascot's home planetoid growing over a long habit streak:
 1) day 1: a tiny bare moss asteroid barely bigger than the mascot, one sprout, a few faint stars;
 2) about a week: slightly bigger planet, a cushion, a few flowers, one glowing mushroom lamp, three bright stars joined into the beginning of a constellation;
 3) about a month: noticeably bigger planet with a small round cottage, a swing tree, a pond, a telescope, a little moon orbiting, a longer constellation;
 4) about a hundred days: a lush, glowing little world with a soft pastel ring around it, two small moons, a pet comet circling, fairy lights, a lighthouse-like beacon, a large finished constellation filling the sky; the mascot looks proud.
