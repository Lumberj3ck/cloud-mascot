Idea: A POCKET WORLD UNDER GLASS. A glass dome terrarium (like a snow globe) sits on a sunny wooden windowsill; inside it is the mascot's whole miniature world: soft rolling lavender-and-mint hills, a tiny pond, a little round door in a hill, a miniature rainbow and a few floating sparkle motes. The mascot sits inside the dome, clearly visible and cute. Outside the window, a blurred dusky-pink city skyline bokeh. Gentle reflections on the glass. Collectible, intimate, home-screen-widget feeling.
