CUSTOMISATION: TERRARIUM ITEM CATALOGUE. A neat item sheet like a cosy game shop: many separate miniature items, each isolated and evenly spaced in a tidy grid on a plain soft cream background with gentle shadows, all in the same soft pastel vinyl-toy style. Items for decorating a tiny world inside a glass terrarium: different glass vessels (dome, sphere, bell jar, hexagonal terrarium) and bases (oak, ceramic, brass, woven), a hill with a round door, a tiny pond, an arched bridge, a mini rainbow, mushrooms, a bench, a swing, a mailbox, a lantern, round bushes, flower clusters, pebble stepping stones, a tiny cabin, a little windmill, and weather effects shown as small tokens (a mini rain cloud, snowflakes, fireflies jar, sparkles). In one corner, three small copies of the mascot each wearing a different tiny accessory: a knitted beanie, a little flower crown, a tiny scarf. No text, no prices, no UI.
